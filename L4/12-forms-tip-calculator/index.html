Level 4 · The page · Task 3

Forms: a tip calculator

Your page: fill it in and press Calculate

Goal

Read what the user typed, work out the tip, and show the answer. This is task 1.3 (strings vs numbers) and 2.2 (conditions) with a real form.

You'll use

  • input.value
  • Number()
  • "submit" event
  • event.preventDefault()
  • .toFixed(2)

Steps

  1. Listen for the form's submit event and call event.preventDefault(). Without it, the page reloads and your result disappears.
  2. Inside calculate(), read the three values and turn them into numbers.
  3. If the bill is empty or not above 0, show "Please enter a bill amount." in #error, clear #result, and stop.
  4. Work out the tip, the total, and the amount each person pays.
  5. Show exactly Tip: $15.00 · Total: $115.00 · Each: $57.50 (for a $100 bill, 15%, 2 people), and clear the error.

.value is always a string. Even from type="number". "100" + 15 is "10015". Wrap it in Number(), just like in task 1.3.

Think about it

  • Why listen for submit on the form instead of click on the button? (Hint: press Enter in an input.)
  • What does Number("") give? Is that a problem for your check in step 3?
Bonus

Live update: also run calculate() on the form's "input" event, so the result changes while the user types. One listener on the form covers all three fields, because events bubble up from the inputs.
